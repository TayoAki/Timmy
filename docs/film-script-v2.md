# Product film, script v2 (Demo2Win "Tell-Show-Tell")

Each part opens on the problem with no software on screen (a type card), shows the feature in the app,
then recaps what it means. About 2:20 at Liam's pace. It covers every feature on the list: the email
assistant (Drive, docs, slides and sheets, drafts and what needs attention, projects and proposals)
and the CRM (finding leads, the cold email list, writing the emails, speed to lead).

Generate as one take in ElevenLabs with the same "Liam" voice and settings, on Multilingual v2 so the
`<break>` tags work. The film is then timed from its word timings.

## Voiceover

```text
If you run a small business, your inbox decides your day. <break time="0.5s" /> Quotes to send. People to chase. Files buried three folders deep. <break time="0.4s" /> And new leads go cold while you're stuck replying. <break time="0.8s" /> What if it was all handled before you sat down? <break time="0.6s" /> This is Timmy. <break time="1.0s" />

Start with the inbox. <break time="0.3s" /> You shouldn't have to read everything to find what matters. <break time="0.5s" /> Each morning, Timmy sorts your mail and ranks what needs you today, with the reason why. <break time="0.4s" /> When something needs a reply, the draft is already written, from your own files and numbers. <break time="0.4s" /> You check it. You send it. Nothing goes out without you. <break time="0.8s" />

Next, the files behind the work. <break time="0.3s" /> Timmy connects to your Drive, files everything into clear folders, and links each email to the files it needs. <break time="0.4s" /> Docs, slides and sheets open right here, and new ones start from a template in one click. <break time="0.8s" />

Then, the deals in flight. <break time="0.3s" /> Every project and proposal has its own page: the tasks, the latest version, and who owes what. <break time="0.4s" /> Change the price once, and the reply, the proposal and the order form update together. <break time="0.8s" />

Now, new customers. <break time="0.3s" /> A good lead list usually eats a whole afternoon. <break time="0.4s" /> Tell Timmy who you're looking for. It finds businesses that fit your offer, scores each one, fills in the contact details, and turns your best fits into a cold email list. <break time="0.8s" />

Then it writes to them. <break time="0.3s" /> Ask for a campaign, and Timmy writes the whole sequence from your offer. <break time="0.4s" /> Each email lands as a draft in your own Gmail, inside safe daily limits. <break time="0.4s" /> You read them. You send them. <break time="0.8s" />

And when a lead finds you first, speed wins. <break time="0.3s" /> Plug in your website form, Typeform, HubSpot or Facebook Lead Ads. <break time="0.4s" /> The moment a lead comes in, Timmy drafts the first reply, and you book the call in one click. <break time="0.8s" />

So your inbox stops deciding your day. <break time="0.4s" /> Timmy sorts it, drafts it, files it, tracks it, and finds your next customers. <break time="0.4s" /> And nothing goes out until you say so. <break time="0.8s" /> Timmy. <break time="0.4s" /> Never drop a ball.
```

## Truth notes

- Demo mode: nothing is sent or connected for real, and the Gmail and Drive connections need the backend, so the film is a pre-release recreation.
- "Connect your Gmail" isn't true today; "Try the demo" is. The owner chooses.
- No vendor names beyond Gmail, Drive and the form sources the product lists (Typeform, HubSpot, Facebook Lead Ads).
